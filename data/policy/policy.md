**Your trust matters. So does your privacy.**

At WOW Superagency, we believe great digital experiences should be built on transparency, responsibility, and respect for personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how WOW Superagency (“WOW,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you visit our websites, contact our team, request a service, or interact with our business.

## Who We Are

WOW Superagency is an integrated business, creative, marketing, and technology agency based in Mississauga, Ontario, Canada.

We bring together specialized services and brands to help businesses build, launch, operate, and grow.

This policy applies to WOW Superagency's website and to other WOW-operated websites and digital experiences that link to this policy, unless a separate privacy notice applies.

## Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, information generated when you interact with our websites, and information received from service providers or other sources where permitted by law.

**Information you provide**

- Name, email address, telephone number, and business details.
- Information submitted through contact, quotation, or consultation forms.
- Project requirements, service interests, and business inquiries.
- Communications and other information you choose to share with our team.

**Information collected automatically**

Depending on the technologies enabled on our websites, we may collect:

- IP address, browser type, device type, and operating system.
- Pages visited, referral sources, and interactions with website content.
- Cookie identifiers and analytics information.
- Technical information used to maintain website performance and security.

We encourage you not to submit sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and an appropriate secure method has been arranged.

## How We Use Your Information

We use personal information for legitimate business purposes and other purposes permitted by applicable law, including to:

- Respond to questions, inquiries, and quotation requests.
- Schedule meetings and provide requested consultations.
- Deliver and manage our services and client relationships.
- Communicate about projects, proposals, and support requests.
- Improve our websites, services, and user experiences.
- Maintain website security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot technical issues.
- Send marketing communications where permitted by law and subject to applicable consent requirements.
- Comply with legal obligations and protect our legal rights.

We do not use personal information for purposes materially different from those described here without providing appropriate notice or obtaining consent where required.

## Cookies & Website Analytics

Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies to support essential functionality, understand website usage, remember preferences, and improve digital experiences.

Depending on the services enabled, these technologies may include:

- **Essential cookies:** Support website functionality and security.
- **Analytics cookies:** Help us understand how visitors use our websites.
- **Preference cookies:** Remember certain settings and choices.
- **Marketing cookies:** Support campaign measurement and relevant communications where enabled.

Where required, we request consent before using non-essential cookies.

You can manage available cookie preferences through our Cookie Settings feature and through your browser controls. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

## When We Share Information

We may share personal information with trusted third parties when necessary to operate our business and provide services.

These may include:

- Website hosting and infrastructure providers.
- Customer relationship management and communication platforms.
- Meeting scheduling and business collaboration tools.
- Analytics and marketing technology providers.
- Professional advisers and other service providers.
- WOW-affiliated brands or specialist teams involved in delivering a requested service.

We may also disclose information when required by law, in connection with a legitimate business transaction, or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

We do not sell personal information in exchange for money. Any use or disclosure that constitutes a sale, sharing, or targeted advertising under applicable privacy law will be addressed in accordance with that law.

## Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

These measures may include appropriate access controls, secure systems, service-provider oversight, and internal handling procedures.

No online service or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. We therefore continually consider security risks and take appropriate steps to address them.

## Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information, our relationship with you, and applicable legal requirements.

When information is no longer required, we take appropriate steps to delete, anonymize, or securely dispose of it.

## International Data Processing

WOW Superagency may work with service providers and technology platforms that process or store information outside your province or country of residence.

When personal information is transferred internationally, it may be subject to the laws of the jurisdiction where it is processed.

Where required by applicable law, we use appropriate contractual, technical, and organizational safeguards for international transfers.

## Your Privacy Choices & Rights

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to:

- Request access to personal information we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
- Request deletion of certain personal information.
- Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.
- Object to or restrict certain uses of your information.
- Request information about how your data is processed or disclosed.
- Submit a complaint to an appropriate privacy regulator.

You may also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe option included in those communications.

To exercise a privacy right, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

## Third-Party Websites & Services

Our websites may contain links to external websites, platforms, or services that WOW Superagency does not operate.

These third parties have their own privacy practices and policies.

We encourage you to review their privacy notices before providing personal information. WOW is not responsible for the privacy practices of independently operated third-party websites.

## Children's Privacy

Our websites and business services are intended primarily for businesses and professional audiences and are not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable privacy laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business, services, technology, or legal obligations.

When we make changes, we will update the “Last updated” date on this page. Where required by law, we will provide additional notice or request consent.

We encourage you to review this page periodically.
